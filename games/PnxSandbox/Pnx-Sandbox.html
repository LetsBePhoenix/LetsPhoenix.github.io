Load a ready-made world – your current one will be replaced. Each world starts with a fitting temperature and an experiment to try. Tip: paused or at 0.5× you can study what happens more closely.

How it works

Pnx-Sandbox is a small physics simulation: every pixel is a material with its own behaviour and temperature. Powders trickle, liquids flow, gases rise – and materials transform when they get hot or cold enough.

Temperature

Every pixel has a temperature. Heat always flows from hot to cold – how fast depends on thermal conductivity: metal and gold conduct very well, wood, air and steam hardly at all. Hot air rises. When a material gets hot or cold enough, it changes state: ice melts, water boils, stone turns into lava, lava solidifies. Flammable things ignite at their ignition temperature.

In the thermal view (T) you see these colours directly. The bottom left of the playing field always shows which material is under the mouse and how warm it is.

💡 Light

Sunlight falls in from above. Air and glass let it through, water dims it slowly (it gets dark deep down), leaves, smoke and oil cast shadows, solid materials block it completely – that is why caves are dark. A little light also scatters sideways into cave entrances.

Light sources: 💡 lamp, torch, fire, lava, molten glass/metal and anything glowing red light up their surroundings. Plants and algae use any light – with a lamp you can grow a garden in a cave or deep under water.

In the light view (L, 💡) you see brightness as colours: black-violet = dark, orange = dim, light yellow = full light. You can switch off shadows and glow in the normal view with 🌗.

🌱 How plants work

Every plant needs three things:

💧 WaterThe roots draw it from moist soil (darker = wetter) and carry it upwards. Water with water or a cloud.
☀️ SunLeaves need light – ideally open sky, but a lamp or a fire nearby helps too. Water dims sunlight, leaves above cast shadows, solid materials block it completely. From it they make energy (⚡).
🌡 WarmthGrowth only between 5 and 38 °C. Below −2 °C the plant freezes, above 40 °C it dries out quickly.

Life cycle: a seed sprouts on moist soil (it does not need sun for that yet – it has a reserve) → root grows down, stem grows up → leaves produce energy → once fully grown a blossom forms → seeds drop out → the blossom wilts into dead leaves → dead leaves rot into soil.

Warning signs: 🟡 yellow-brown = thirsty, ⚪ pale = too little sun/energy, 🟤 dead leaves = died. Move the mouse over a plant – at the bottom left you see water, energy, light and what it is missing right now.

Nature & cycles

Water: water slowly evaporates, steam rises, forms clouds at the top and rains down. Soil soaks up rain (it gets darker) – too much water turns it into mud.

Aquatic life: floating algae multiply quickly as long as some light reaches them – even in fairly deep water or next to a lamp. Fish eat algae, multiply when they are full and starve without food.

Experiments to try

🧯 Oil fireLight a puddle of oil and pour water on it – it splashes burning oil around! Better let it burn out.
🌳 Giant treeWater a wide mound of soil well and put a tree seed on it. It grows with a thick trunk, branches and a big crown.
🐦 Birds sowPut birds above a garden with flowers. They eat seeds and drop them somewhere else – that is how new plants grow.
🐜 AnthillPut a few ants on soil. They dig tunnels and carry the soil upwards. A blob of honey attracts them.
💎 DiamondPut charcoal on the ground and let lightning strike – part of it turns into diamond.
🧂 Growing crystalsPut a salt crystal into a basin of salt water – it keeps growing in cubes.
🧲 MagnetChoose the magnet under Tools and drag sand, water or gases somewhere else. The brush size sets the grab area. Solid walls block the movement; paused, you can work very precisely.
🌀 MixingChoose the Mixing Tool under Tools and drag it over powders, liquids or gases. The brush size sets the mixing area; solid walls stay intact. Also works while paused.
💨 WindTake the wind tool and drag it over smoke, sand or a fire – everything gets blown away.
🏖 SandcastlePour a little water over a pile of sand – wet sand sticks and holds steeper shapes. In the sun it dries again.
💨 Shock wavePut a piece of TNT into a pile of sand and ignite it – the shock wave hurls the sand far away.
🌋 Making obsidianPour lava into a basin of water. Where the lava cools down suddenly, black obsidian forms – and lots of steam.
🌸 GardenHeap up soil, water it, sprinkle seeds on top. Move the mouse over the seedlings to see how they are doing – water, sun and energy are shown.
🐟 AquariumA water basin with a few fish and floating algae. The fish eat the algae and multiply – without food they starve.
🌿 Underwater forestPaint seaweed on the bottom of a water basin. It grows upwards as long as enough light reaches it.
🛢 Burning seaPour oil onto water and light it – it keeps burning on the water surface.
🕳 Black holePlace a black hole next to a pile of sand. It sucks in matter in a spiral, grows and pulls ever harder. Two holes merge.
🚰 WaterworksPut a spring at the top and a drain at the bottom – in between you can build basins and waterfalls.
🏠 Build a houseUse the “Frame” drawing mode for brick walls and “Fill” for concrete floors.
🧽 Sponge testPut a sponge into a puddle. It soaks itself full and drips when it is saturated.
🌧 Water cyclePut heaters under a water basin. The steam rises, forms clouds at the top and rains down again.
❄️ BlizzardSet the ambient temperature to −20 °C and paint steam or clouds – it snows, and water freezes from the top.
⚡ Lightning glassPile up some sand and let the lightning tool strike. The sand melts into glass.
🔥 Casting metalHeat metal with the heat tool above 1550 °C – it melts, flows and solidifies again.
🌵 DroughtSet the ambient temperature to 40 °C and leave a garden without water – the plants wilt and crumble into dead leaves.
🧱 Firing bricksPut clay on a heater. From 900 °C it turns into solid, heat-resistant bricks.
💥 Chain reactionBuild a row of TNT, connect it with a fuse and put a torch at the end.
🎆 FireworksScatter fireworks on the ground and light them – the rockets rise and burst into colourful sparks.
🌲 Plant a forestPile up a long mound of soil, water it and scatter tree seeds. Later, mushrooms in the shade decompose the dead leaves.
🐝 Bee colonyPlant flowers (seeds) and put a beehive next to them. The bees pollinate the blossoms and fill the hive – honey drips out underneath.
🕳 No sunLet seeds sprout in a closed cave: they grow from their reserve, turn pale and die without sun.
🌻 Towards the sunLet plants sprout at the edge of an overhang – they grow at an angle towards open sky.
🪱 CompostPut dead leaves on soil with a few worms – they turn it into moist humus.
🪣 SievingStretch a 1-pixel-thick mesh and tip a sand-gravel mix onto it: the sand trickles through, the gravel stays on top.
🔥 OvenBuild a box of insulation with a heater inside – the heat stays in.
🌋 Volcanic islandPut a volcano on the sea floor. With every eruption an island of obsidian and stone grows.
